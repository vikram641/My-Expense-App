# Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

_Last updated: August 2026_

Expense Tracker ("the app") is developed and maintained by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you register or log in, we collect your name and email address to create and secure your account. Authentication is handled via JWT tokens.

**Expense and budget data.** Expenses, categories, notes, dates, and budget amounts you enter are stored on our servers and on your device (for offline access) so the app can sync and display your data across sessions.

**Device and usage data.** We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage (e.g., screens visited, feature usage) in aggregate, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications (e.g., budget alerts), which requires a device notification token.

## How We Use This Information

- To provide core app functionality — storing and syncing your expenses and budgets
- To authenticate your account and keep your data secure
- To send you budget and sync-related notifications
- To understand app usage and improve the app (via aggregated analytics)

We do not sell your personal information. Expense and budget data you enter is not shared with third parties except as necessary to provide the service (e.g., our hosting/backend infrastructure).

## Third-Party Services

The app uses:
- **Firebase (Google)** — Analytics and Cloud Messaging. Data handled by Firebase is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Storage & Security

Expense and account data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored on our backend servers. A local copy is cached on your device using an offline-first database to support offline use.

## Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will remove your data within a reasonable time after verifying your request.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
